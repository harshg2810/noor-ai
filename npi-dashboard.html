How this works & what the numbers mean
  • Where the data comes from: the public CMS NPPES registry API (npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov). Every search hits it live from your browser.
  • "NPI issued" is the enumeration date — the day that provider or clinic was granted its National Provider Identifier. A recent date usually means a newly opened practice or a provider who just started billing in the US.
  • Year filtering happens here, not at the API. The registry has no date filter, so the dashboard loads every matching record and groups it by enumeration year.
  • 1,200-record ceiling: the API returns at most 1,200 records per search (200 per page, 6 pages). If you hit it, narrow the search by city, ZIP, or specialty for accurate year counts.
  • Addresses shown are the practice location, not the mailing address.
